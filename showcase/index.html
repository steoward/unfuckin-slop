Monobloc magnesium. Zero internal acoustic reflection.

Traditional audio gear is assembled from stamped sheet metal that flexes and resonates under acoustic pressure. Model 01 starts as an eight-pound block of AZ91D magnesium, CNC-milled over four hours into a unified acoustic chassis.
