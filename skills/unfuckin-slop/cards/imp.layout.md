# Layout

- **One focal point per viewport.** If two things compete, neither wins. Decide the order the eye moves in.
- **Spacing follows a scale** (4 or 8 base) and repeats. Random 13px/27px gaps are the fastest way to look assembled rather than designed.
- **Tight groups, generous separation, more space above a heading than below it.** A heading belongs to the content under it, not the block above.
- **Align to something.** A grid, an optical edge, a shared baseline. Misalignment by 2px reads as carelessness even when nobody can name it.
- **Density is a mode decision.** Operate screens earn density and scanability; Persuade screens earn air. Do not split the difference.
- **Cards are a container of last resort.** Prefer typographic grouping, rules, or a table. Nested cards are always wrong.
- **Bento only when content is heterogeneous.** Equal tiles with equal content is a grid pretending to be a composition.

# Composition moves that are not slop

- An asymmetric split (7/5, 8/4) with a deliberate imbalance.
- A full-bleed element that breaks the container exactly once.
- A type-led section with no imagery at all.
- A single oversized number or word carrying the section.

Each of these is a decision. Two of them on one page is a system; five is noise.
