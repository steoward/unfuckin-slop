# Polish order

Fix in this order. Later steps are wasted if an earlier one moves the layout.

1. **Structure.** Remove a section, a card row, or a competing focal point. Subtraction first.
2. **Spacing.** Apply the scale; fix the space above headings; align the optical edges.
3. **Type.** Scale steps, measure, tracking, line-height, tabular numerals.
4. **Colour.** Contrast pairs, tinted neutrals, dark mode surfaces, focus ring colour.
5. **States.** Hover, focus-visible, disabled, loading, error, empty.
6. **Motion.** One authored moment; remove the rest; check reduced motion.
7. **Copy.** Labels, errors, empty states, the banned phrases.
8. **Browser surfaces.** Selection, caret, scrollbar, underline offset.

# Stop rule

Polish ends when the craft floor is green and the committed idea is visible without explanation. Beyond that you are gold-plating, and gold plating on a weak structure makes the weakness more obvious.

Do not change the direction during polish. If the direction is wrong, say so and stop; polishing a discarded look is the most expensive way to avoid a decision.
