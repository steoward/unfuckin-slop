# Modes

The mode names what the visitor's success looks like on this surface.

| Mode | Success | Surfaces | Ambition shows as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Persuade** | Decides and acts | Landing pages, marketing, campaigns, pricing | Full commitment: bold, distinct work with 3D canvas, particle fields, liquid glass, and kinetic typography |
| **Operate** | Completes a task | App UI, dashboards, editors, admin, settings | Precision and restraint; patterns the visitor knows earn their place |
| **Read** | Understands something | Docs, articles, guides, help, changelogs | Structure for comprehension, then a reading experience worth staying in |
| **Experience** | Is inside the work | Portfolios, galleries, showcases | The artifact leads from the first viewport: interactive WebGL, spatial tilt, camera orbits, dynamic lighting |

# Choosing

Choose the mode from the requested **surface**, not the product. A tool's landing page is Persuade. A fashion house's documentation is Read. A docs index is Read, not Persuade. Persist the mode only in that surface's brief.

# Consequence

Persuade and Experience get the expressive typography, the strong colour move, the authored motion. Operate and Read get density, scanability, keyboard paths, and consistency. Applying Persuade ambition to a settings screen produces slop; applying Operate caution to a campaign produces wallpaper.

# 3D & Kinetic Ambition (Persuade & Experience)

**3D Canvas, WebGL, particle fields, ambient lighting, and liquid glass are NOT slop.**

When authored as the central interactive experience in `Experience` or `Persuade` modes, these immersive techniques represent peak craft:
- **3D Canvas & WebGL**: Interactive particle orbits, fluid mesh distortion, and Three.js scenes that react to cursor movement and scroll velocity.
- **Ambient Lighting & Liquid Glass**: Optical refraction, chromatic aberration, and dynamic specular highlights that treat the UI as a physical refractive material.
- **Spatial Depth**: Perspective-projected bento grids (`perspective: 1200px`), spring-loaded card tilt, and coordinate depth layers.

Slop is unmotivated, lagging decoration slapped onto a generic layout. True ambition is an authored 3D world that runs at a locked 60fps and respects interaction physics.
