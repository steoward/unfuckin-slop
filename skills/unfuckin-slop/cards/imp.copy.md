# Copy

- **Controls name their action.** "Save changes", not "Submit" or "Continue". The label is a promise about what happens next.
- **Errors name the problem and the recovery**, in that order, in plain language. No codes alone, no blame, no "oops".
- **Every word earns its place.** No intros that restate the heading, no headings that restate the page title, no "Welcome to" above a product name.
- **Sentence case.** Title Case On Every Label reads as a marketing deck, and ALL CAPS reads as a warning.
- **Specific beats clever.** "Sync paused: no connection" beats "Oops! Something hiccuped."
- **No em dashes.** Use commas, colons, semicolons, periods, or parentheses. Also not `--`.
- **No jargon the user did not bring.** If the product says "instances", the user says "servers", use "servers".
- **Numbers carry units and precision honestly.** "1.2 s", "3 of 12", "~40 MB". Not "blazing fast".
- **Placeholders are not labels.** They disappear exactly when the user needs them.

# Banned phrases

"Elevate your workflow." "Seamlessly integrate." "Unlock the power of." "Click here." "Learn more" as the only link text. "Oops!" "Something went wrong." "Revolutionary." Any sentence with two of: empower, streamline, leverage, robust, cutting-edge.
