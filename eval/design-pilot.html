Two design briefs, four prototypes

Compare the two solutions to the same brief. Select a recording or inspect a rights record inside either prototype. All content is fictional. The audio previews are silent.

Method and observations

Four independent agent runs implemented the same content and constraints within each pair. One used selected craft cards. One also received the hybrid retriever's three real catalog descriptions and the design-direction workflow. Neither used external media. A separate evaluator saw eight anonymized screenshots at 390 and 1200 pixels with no implementation labels. Generation varied between runs. This pilot compares workflows and does not isolate the encoder or establish a general quality score.

Field recordings. A used references and cards. The evaluator preferred its dot-matrix player for identity, and B (cards) for selection hierarchy, narrow layout and overall usability.

Editorial rights. B used references and cards. Its blocker-first queue and image inspector won for hierarchy, identity and overall clarity. A (cards) retained clearer column labels on mobile.
